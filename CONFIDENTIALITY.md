# Confidentiality and anonymization

This public repository was rebuilt to prevent disclosure of confidential
industrial information.

It intentionally excludes:

- original and processed industrial spreadsheets;
- internal asset codes and equipment descriptions;
- work-order and maintenance-history details;
- record-level reconciliation outputs;
- dashboards and legacy thesis artifacts; and
- aggregate tables and figures derived from the confidential records; and
- file metadata inherited from the industrial source files.

The identifiers in `data/synthetic/synthetic_asset_events.csv` use the
`SYN-####` pattern and were generated solely for demonstration. No synthetic
row corresponds to an original operational record.

Only synthetic demonstration outputs may be generated by the public workflow.
